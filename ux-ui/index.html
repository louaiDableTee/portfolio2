Understood · UX/UI and messaging

One product, two very different buyers.

The skill

Turn a product description into a promise a specific person recognises, then clear the path to the action.

The problem

An unclear entry point. No separation between the recruiter journey and the sales journey. Vague calls to action. A long, confusing mobile page.

The proof

The new homepage promise and its call to action are live on worku.tn today.

01 · The audit

Where the journey broke.

I mapped the five stages a visitor passes through, then marked where each one loses them.

  • Unclear entryThe first screen spoke to everyone, so it spoke to no one.
  • No segmentationRecruiters and sales teams shared one path.
  • Undemonstrated productThe four steps of the product were never shown in order.
  • Vague actionsCommencer maintenant didn’t say what happened next.

04 · Separate journeys

A recruiter and a sales rep are not the same visitor.

They arrive with different words and different jobs. One page could not serve both, so I built four.

2audiences separated: recruiters and sales teams

4dedicated pages, up from one general page

Same layout, same product, deliberately different words. Read the two headlines against each other.

The other two pages complete the four and are live to read: recherche-profils for searching profiles and companies, and enrichissement-contacts for unlocking a verified email or mobile. They support the count without asking you to look at two more near-identical screenshots.

05 · Mobile

Text before decoration.

  1. Text before illustrations. The message arrives before the artwork.
  2. Buttons higher. The action sits where a thumb already is.
  3. Shorter page. Less scrolling to reach the same decision.

Honest limits

What this redesign does not prove

  • No conversion claim. There was no continuous, complete tracking in place during the internship, so I cannot show a conversion lift and do not claim one.
  • No A/B test. These were reasoned design decisions applied to a live product, not a controlled experiment.
  • The message is verifiable, the impact is not. You can load worku.tn and read the new promise. That is the part I can prove.
