Measured · Google Search Console

The report that says why pages never arrive.

The skill

Use Search Console as a control panel, not a scoreboard. Find what blocks visibility before spending anything on content.

The problem

Pages were being crawled and then not indexed. Legacy routes were throwing 404s. Nobody was reading the coverage data.

The proof

Two Search Console captures below, straight from the property for worku.tn.

What I did with it

Daily, not occasionally.

  1. Monitored indexing and coverage. Tracked which pages moved between states and why.
  2. Submitted and validated sitemaps. Confirmed acceptance rather than assuming it.
  3. Inspected URLs. Checked individual pages against Google’s own view of them.
  4. Ran live tests. Verified the current state of a page rather than the cached one.
  5. Diagnosed old-route errors. Separated genuine technical faults from expected non-indexable URLs.

How to read those four numbers

Position 7.1 is not a win

  • The volume is tiny and the queries are mostly branded. An average position of 7.1 across 673 impressions, largely from people already searching for Worku by name, tells you almost nothing about competitive visibility. I include it as the starting state of the property and nothing more.
  • These are initial-state figures. They are not a result of my work, and I do not present them as one.
