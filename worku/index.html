Flagship case study · February to July 2026

One product. Six months. Rebuilt end to end.

Worku is a B2B SaaS platform that finds professional profiles and returns verified contact details. It serves two audiences, recruiters and sales teams. I joined as Product & Growth for my master’s internship.

The company

Worku is the brand of Linteern SARL, founded 2024, based in La Marsa, Tunis. The site is worku.tn.

The situation

The product had pivoted from an applicant tracking system to a sourcing and prospecting platform. The website had not caught up.

My role

Product & Growth, February to July 2026, reporting to the CTO, M. Salem Dahmani.

Research & lead generation

Who we were actually talking to.

Before rewriting a page I mapped the two audiences and the vocabulary each one uses.

  1. ICP and persona research. Separated the recruiter buyer from the sales buyer, since they search with different words.
  2. Competitor analysis. Studied how comparable sourcing tools position and price themselves.
  3. Outreach angles. Tested lead-generation approaches against each persona.
  4. Product testing. Used the sourcing tool as a user, documented relevance and filtering issues, and fed them back to the technical team.

Read this before you read the numbers

What this case study does not claim

  • Traffic did not increase. The changes went live in April. The window was far too short to attribute any traffic movement, so I make no traffic claim at all. This is foundation work.
  • The 403 was a manual test. It revealed a real risk to crawl access, not a proven permanent block.
  • Average position 7.1 sits on very low volume. It came from 673 impressions, mostly brand queries. On its own it means little.
  • The follower growth has several causes. Posts contributed, and so did invitations, outreach and the project growing. I cannot attribute it to content alone.
  • There are no GA4 results. I ran basic checks only. I deliberately kept GA4 numbers out of my thesis, and they stay out of this portfolio.
