What I actually did with GA4 on Worku

  • Basic visibility and performance checks. That is the accurate description, and it is the same wording that appears on my CV.
  • There was no continuous, complete tracking in place during the internship.
  • I deliberately kept GA4 numbers out of my thesis. The data was not solid enough to carry a conclusion, so I did not let it.
  • There is no GA4 result on this site, and there will not be one. No dashboard, no invented figures, no chart built from numbers I cannot stand behind.

The finding

The gap was the finding.

Not being able to measure the work was itself the most useful thing I learned about the product.

The skill

Recognise when a measurement setup cannot answer the question being asked of it, and say so.

The problem

Site changes went live in April. Without continuous tracking and consistent campaign tagging, no change could be attributed to anything.

The response

I reported the gap rather than working around it, and framed the work as foundations instead of results.

Proposal, not result

How I would close it.

This is what I would put in place on day one of a role like this. It was not implemented on Worku.

  1. Define the KPIs before the tooling. Decide which few numbers a decision actually depends on, so the setup serves a question instead of collecting everything.
  2. Separate the two audiences in reporting. Recruiter and sales journeys need to be readable apart, or the averages hide both.
  3. Agree a UTM convention and write it down. One naming scheme, applied by everyone, or campaign data is unusable within a month.
  4. Set the key events explicitly. Signing up for a free search is the moment that matters, so it should be a deliberate event, not an inferred one.
  5. Pair GA4 with Search Console. One shows what people do on the site, the other shows how they got there. Neither is complete alone.
  6. Fix a baseline period before shipping changes. Without a clean before, there is no honest after.

To be explicit

Everything above is a plan

  • None of this was deployed on Worku. It is how I would approach the measurement problem, presented as method. Treat it as thinking, not as a delivered result.
